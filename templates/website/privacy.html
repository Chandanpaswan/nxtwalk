Information is stored in the website's database and retained only as needed for the purposes above, to meet applicable legal obligations, or to resolve an enquiry or service relationship.

You may request access, correction or deletion of information you have provided, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-keeping requirements. To make a privacy request, use the contact form.

This policy may be updated as the website or applicable requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest version.
